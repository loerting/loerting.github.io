About the studio

Loerting Games was founded by Leonhard Kohl-Lörting, who studied computer science at the University of Innsbruck.

Vena started as a game jam prototype that won Godot Wild Jam #85. It comes out on Steam on 9 November 2026, and the demo is free to play now. Fish Don’t Return, a programming game set in the Mariana Trench, comes next.

For press, review keys or anything else, send an email.
