About Loerting Games

Loerting Games is the shared name under which Alexander and Leonhard Kohl-Lörting present their games. Each game is developed and published by one of the two alone: What the Buck?! by Alexander, Vena and Fish Don’t Return by Leonhard. Loerting Games itself is not a company or a publisher.

For press, review keys or anything else, send an email.

If you ask for a review key, please write from the email address listed on your site or channel, or include other proof that you run it.
