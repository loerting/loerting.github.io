An automation roguelike where every run fits in one sitting. Place hexagonal tiles to grow a production organism, roll dice in the shop and survive nine phases as the Nexus gets hungrier.

Out 9 November 2026 Windows, macOS, Linux and Steam Deck

The depth of an automation game, in self-contained roguelike runs of 15 to 50 minutes.

How a run goes

What’s in it

Easy to pick up and hard to master. Expect a hundred hours or more to see all of it.

In Vena, you can

  • Try to break the game with wild strategies and hunt for the perfect synergy.
  • Optimise your organism by reading the statistics and refining its flow and layout.
  • Get better every run. No two are the same, since every session makes you adapt to new perks and tiles.
  • Progress through seven game modes and five difficulty tiers.
  • Relax to sound design composed with world instruments, and take in the detailed pixel art.
  • Build the perfect map without any goals in Sandbox mode, or take on the toughest challenge.

Vena contains

  • 85+ rotatable hex tiles across five rarities (base, common, rare, mythic and legendary), each with its own effect and its own pixel artwork.
  • 92+ perks that break the rules, enable wild strategies or unlock powerful abilities.
  • 20+ unlockable skins, earned by completing challenges and beating high difficulties.
  • Seven game modes and five difficulties, all of them playable as Endless runs.
  • StandardManage your resources and survive all nine phases.
  • SandboxInfinite resources and no doom. Build the perfect organism.
  • CompactA shorter session on a small map with a reduced deck.
  • StarA star-shaped map that asks for different planning.
  • IslandsSix separate map pieces, joined through portals.
  • SpiralBuild along a spiral out from the centre.
  • DailyNew starting conditions every day.
  • EndlessEvery mode can keep going after the last phase.
