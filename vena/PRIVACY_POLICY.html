Privacy Policy for Vena

Last updated: 16 September 2026

Vena (“the game”) is developed and published by Leonhard Kohl-Lörting (“I”, “me”). This policy explains what the Android version of Vena does with your data.

Short version

Vena does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not need an internet connection to play.

Data stored on your device

Vena saves the following locally, in the app’s private storage:

  • your game progress and save files
  • your settings (language, audio volume, controls, graphics options)
  • your unlocked skins and achievements
  • screenshots you choose to take with the in-game screenshot function

This data never leaves your device. It is not readable by me or by anyone else. If you uninstall Vena, Android deletes it along with the app.

Data sent off your device

None. Vena makes no network requests of its own and contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or attribution SDKs.

Links that open outside the game

Vena has buttons that open external pages in your browser or in another app. Opening one is always your choice, and once you leave the game you are covered by that service’s own privacy policy, not this one. Those destinations are:

  • Steam: store.steampowered.com
  • Discord: discord.gg
  • YouTube: www.youtube.com
  • Instagram: www.instagram.com
  • a Google Forms feedback form, if you choose to send feedback. Anything you type into that form is processed by Google under Google’s privacy policy.

Purchases

Vena is a paid app. Your purchase, and any free trial offered through Google Play, is handled entirely by Google Play. I never see your payment details. Google’s handling of that transaction is covered by the Google Play Terms of Service and Google’s privacy policy.

Children

Vena contains no data collection, no ads and no social features, so there is nothing for it to collect from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL and the date at the top will change.
